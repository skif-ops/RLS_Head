# Issue #216: CI581 native import attempt

This directory preserves the second Altium Designer 26.10.1 Trial import attempt. The `system-carrier-altium-import` artifact 12262810614 passed the 112-file package verification and all 52 current-source bindings before import. The root `EVT_SYS_CARRIER.kicad_sch` and `SystemCarrier_Local.kicad_sym` came from that qualified package. `attempt_import_receipt.json` identifies the package and exact native snapshot hashes; `attempt_status.json` lists the retained file hashes and the blocked disposition.

Altium imported eight genuine SchDoc documents. A blank PcbDoc was created in the newly imported CI581 project, the prior native PcbLib was attached, and the ECO executed. The 29 KiCad footprint files in the CI577 and CI581 packages were byte-identical, which is why that PcbLib was reused. The saved PCB contains 92 components, 48 nets, 525 pads and 1,054 track primitives. `native_file_preflight.json` passes the binary-container checks. The `native_snapshot/` files are diagnostic evidence, **not** the canonical `../native/` design.

The Altium Automatic compilation produced **54 errors and 169 warnings**. The executed ECO logged 92 component additions, 48 net additions and 148 pin-to-net operations. `eco_endpoint_comparison.json` compares those operations with the qualified KiCad netlist: only 147 distinct KiCad endpoints received logged PCB net assignments out of 483; 188 unassigned endpoints belong to multi-endpoint KiCad nets. Most seriously, the Altium `3V3` net contains endpoints from 12 distinct KiCad partitions, including `GND` and Ethernet signals. `RADAR_12V` contains endpoints from six partitions, including `3V3` and `GND`. These are log-derived observations; a direct native netlist audit has not passed.

An unqualified local experiment changed the orientation of generated child hierarchy labels. KiCad ERC remained at zero and its 483-endpoint baseline matched CI581, but Altium still reported 50 errors and 141 warnings. The generator experiment was reverted. Only its raw Altium messages and KiCad check results are kept under `orientation_probe/`; it is not an import candidate.

Two further unqualified diagnostics isolated the child hierarchy wires. With 1.27 mm wires only at the `3V3` child ports, KiCad ERC had zero violations and its 483 physical netlist endpoints matched CI581 exactly, but Altium Automatic compilation still reported 54 errors and 169 warnings. Keeping just one such wire, at `U1` pin 23, produced the same 54/169 result. Explicit Altium Hierarchical net identifier scope also produced 54/169 for that one-wire variant, including the erroneous `3V3` merger with Ethernet sheet entries. With that scope and "Allow Ports to Name Nets" disabled, the count fell to 49 errors and 169 warnings; the `3V3`/Ethernet sheet-entry merge remained. Raw messages and KiCad checks are retained under `hierarchy_probe/`. Neither variant was qualified or promoted; the generator was restored, and current-source binding passed 52/52 afterward.

A final unqualified geometry diagnostic doubled the root sheet-entry pitch from 2.54 to 5.08 mm and enlarged the root sheet symbols. KiCad ERC still found zero violations and the 483 physical endpoints remained unchanged. Altium Automatic compilation reported **54 errors and 221 warnings**, including the same `3V3`/Ethernet sheet-entry merger; the larger sheets added off-sheet warnings. The wide-pitch generator change was reverted, and current-source binding again passed 52/52. Its raw log and KiCad reports are under `hierarchy_probe/`.

The qualified CI581 attempt is blocked by native shorted and missing connections. The locked EVT mechanical datum, routing stack and power routing contracts have not been instantiated on this PCB. Native audit, BOM, netlist, hierarchy and no-mmWave checks, followed by `ALTIUM_EQUIVALENCE_GATE`, remain required. Routing authority and production authority stay `NOT_AUTHORIZED`; issue #216 remains open.
